How Blockchain Technology Works

Blockchain is a decentralized, cryptographically secured architecture designed to establish data integrity and trust across distributed systems.

01

Distributed Ledger Technology

Transactions are cryptographically recorded across a peer-to-peer network of independent nodes, eliminating single points of failure.

02

Immutable Record-Keeping

Once verified and added to a block, transaction data is permanently linked and cannot be altered or retroactively deleted.

03

Smart Contract Automation

Self-executing code logic runs directly on-chain, automatically enforcing rules and agreements when predefined conditions are met.

04

Consensus Mechanisms

Distributed consensus protocols ensure total network state agreement across independent participants without central authorities.

05

Cryptographic Security

Asymmetric key pairs and cryptographic hashing secure identity, transaction validity, and data integrity end-to-end.

06

Transparent Auditability

Event logs provide a verifiable, tamper-evident audit trail accessible in real time across ecosystem participants.

Architecture

Public, Private & Permissioned Chains

Blockchain architectures range from open public networks to permissioned enterprise ledgers. Distributed ledgers provide cryptographic verification where multi-party trust, provenance, and data immutability are paramount.
