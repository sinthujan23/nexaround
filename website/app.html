Capabilities

What makes it work

01

Real-time AR landmark identification

Point the camera at a facade or monument and see what it is straight away — no searching, no typing.

02

Neva, your AI travel companion

Ask out loud or in text. Neva knows where you are, what is in frame, and how the trip has gone so far.

03

Location recommendation engine

Nearby places ranked by how you actually explore, not by who paid to be listed first.

04

Location caching for offline use

Save an area before you go and keep identification and routing working with no signal at all.

Meet Neva

Do not explore alone

Search gives you ten results and asks you to choose. Neva gives you one good answer and the reason behind it — built for the two minutes you spend on a corner deciding what to do next.

  • Understands what is in frame, not only where you are
  • Speaks plainly, without the guidebook voice
  • Carries context across the whole trip
  • Keeps working when the connection does not

How it works

From raised phone
to useful answer

01

You raise the phone

The camera opens into AR mode and detection begins immediately. Nothing to tap, nothing to search.

02

Vision identifies what is in frame

Landmarks and points of interest are matched against the index, each with a confidence score attached.

03

Neva adds the story

Recognition gives you a name. Neva gives you the reason it matters, and answers whatever you ask next.

04

Recommendations follow you forward

What you scanned informs what comes next, and the whole set is cached for when the signal drops.
