The Brand Story

The story behind nexARound

The name nexARound combines three powerful ideas:

• nex(t)

Your next adventure, your next discovery, your next unforgettable moment.

• ARound

Everything happening around you, from hidden cafés and scenic viewpoints to local festivals, authentic food, and unforgettable local experiences with the inbuilt layered AR.

• Augmented Reality (AR)

Simply spin your phone around to instantly discover everything around you. Attractions, restaurants, cafés, shopping, hidden gems, experiences, accommodations, transport, and essential services are intelligently categorized and overlaid onto your surroundings, making it easy to understand what's nearby at a glance. Whether you've just arrived in a new city or are exploring a familiar neighborhood, nexARound helps you orient yourself in seconds, creating an immediate sense of connection, confidence, and familiarity. Instead of wondering where to go next, you can simply look around and let the world reveal itself.

Together, they define our mission:

Helping you discover what's next around you.

nexARound is an AI-powered Tourism Companion that transforms the way people explore destinations across Europe, Latin America, North America, and Asia. Instead of simply showing places on a map, it understands your location, mood, weather, available time, budget, and interests to create intelligent, personalized itineraries that evolve with your journey.

Whether you're discovering hidden gems, enjoying authentic local cuisine, planning a scenic drive, or exploring destinations through intelligent recommendations and immersive AR, nexARound helps you experience every place in a way that feels personal, effortless, and unforgettable.

Every recommendation is designed to answer one simple question:

"What can I discover next around me, right here, right now?"

Because travel isn't just about reaching a destination.
It's about discovering moments that become lifelong memories.
