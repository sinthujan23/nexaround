# Editorial plan

## Why this site exists

Travelpayouts keeps Booking.com, Agoda, Trip.com, Expedia, Viator and GetYourGuide locked
until a project shows **original travel content** and **1,000+ monthly visitors for three
months in a row**. nexaround.com is the company site and can't show either. This site is
built to get there, and to send readers to the app.

**Angle:** remote and unusual places, covered with practical, checked detail that other
sites don't have: measured legs, fuel gaps, free stops with coordinates, permits, costs.

## The five sections

| Section | What goes in it |
| --- | --- |
| Road trips | Long remote roads, leg by leg |
| Extreme places | Coldest, wettest, highest, most isolated places people live |
| Remote villages | Villages at the end of the road |
| Free stops | Lookouts, rest areas, ruins, springs that cost nothing (often spun off a road trip) |
| Festivals | Festivals and cultural events: when they happen, what's special, how to be there (added 19 October 2026) |
| Know-how | Permits, fuel range, water, no-signal planning, insurance |

## How a guide is made

1. **Research and draft (Claude).** Facts with sources, a route measured with
   `scripts/measure_route.py`, freely licensed photos with credits, and a `todo` list of what
   only a person can do. The guide starts as `draft: true`.
2. **Edit (team).** Work through the `todo` list. Every guide needs at least:
   - one first-hand detail or quote (our own trip, a traveller, a local business), and
   - the five key facts re-checked against the listed sources.
   Then set `draft: false`.
3. **Publish.** Build (see README).
4. **Share.** One Pinterest pin per guide. Answer matching questions on Reddit or forums
   where links are allowed.

**Rules**
- No unedited AI text is ever published. The editorial policy page says so publicly.
- Every number has a source or our own measurement, and a "checked" date.
- Photos are ours, or under a free licence with the credit shown. No AI images of real places.
- Recommend a partner only where it genuinely helps the trip.

## Article shapes

- **Road trip:** intro → map → direct vs alternative → fuel gaps → where to sleep →
  free stops → clocks, borders, signal → driving safely → when to go → costs → leg table → sources.
- **Place profile:** intro → why it's unique → how to get there → when to go → where to
  stay → costs → what to know (respect, safety) → sources.
- **Free stops / Know-how:** shorter, built from the same blocks.
