## Surviving the cold

At -30°C, ordinary winter coats will not keep you warm for more than twenty minutes. The key is strict multi-layering:

- **Base layer:** Heavyweight thermal merino wool top and bottom.
- **Mid layer:** Fleece sweater, followed by a thick down puffer jacket or vest.
- **Outer shell:** Windproof, waterproof heavy down parka with an insulated hood.
- **Feet:** Thick wool socks combined with insulated winter boots (rated to -40°C). Normal city sneakers will freeze solid through the rubber sole within minutes.
- **Battery preservation:** Smartphone and camera lithium-ion batteries lose charge rapidly in this temperature. Keep phones inside an inner chest pocket next to your body heat, and carry portable hand-warmers taped to power banks.

## Getting there

High-speed trains (*Gaotie*) run directly from Beijing Chaoyang or Beijing Railway Station to Harbin West in just under five hours. Domestic flights land at Harbin Taiping International Airport (HRB), from which airport express buses run directly into the city centre.
