## The rules of the road

- **Tires:** The Dempster's crushed shale rock has sharp, flint-like edges that shred passenger car tires. You need light-truck (LT) all-terrain tires with reinforced sidewalls, and carrying **two mounted full-sized spare tires** is strongly recommended.
- **The Eagle Plains fuel gap:** There is zero fuel or services for 370 km between the Klondike corner and Eagle Plains. Verify your vehicle range before leaving the pavement.
- **River ferries:** The Peel River (at km 539) and the Mackenzie River at Tsiigehtchic (km 608) are crossed by free government cable ferries from June to late October. During freeze-up (November) and spring break-up (May), both ferries cease operating until ice roads freeze solid.
