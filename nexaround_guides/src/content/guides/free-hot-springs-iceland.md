## The best natural hot springs

1. **Reykjadalur ("Steam Valley"):**
   Located near the town of Hveragerði, just 45 minutes from Reykjavík. A scenic 3.5 km uphill trail (approx. 45–60 mins hike) passes bubbling mud pools and boiling vents before arriving at a crystal-clear mountain river. The water temperature warms up as you walk further upstream—find your sweet spot around 38°C.
2. **Hellulaug (Westfjords):**
   Perched just below Route 60 near Flókalundur, this natural stone basin holds comfortable 38°C water with sweeping views over the ocean waves of the fjord.
3. **Etiquette:**
   - Always test the water with your hand before stepping in; geothermal inflow temperatures can fluctuate dangerously.
   - There are no lockers, and changing screens are open-air wooden dividers. Bring a warm robe or poncho and a waterproof bag.
   - Leave no trace: fragile volcanic moss takes decades to recover if trampled. Carry out all trash.
