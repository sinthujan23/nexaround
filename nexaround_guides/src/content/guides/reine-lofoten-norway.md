## Climbing Reinebringen

The hike up Reinebringen was once an eroding, hazardous scramble. Between 2016 and 2019, Nepalese Sherpas built a staircase of 1,560 granite steps:
- **Duration:** 1 to 1.5 hours up, 45 minutes down.
- **Safety:** Do not attempt in icy winter conditions without microspikes or crampons. In summer, stick strictly to the stone steps to prevent rockfalls onto hikers below.
- **The reward:** At the knife-edge crest, the entire Reinefjord, the bridges of the E10, and the distant peaks of the Lofoten Wall open up in a 360-degree vista.

## Practical travel details

- **Getting there:** Fly into Leknes (LKN) or Svolvær (SVJ) via Widerøe turboprop from Bodø, then drive south along the E10. Alternatively, take the car ferry from Bodø to Moskenes (just 5 km south of Reine).
- **Staying in a Rorbu:** The traditional cod-fishermen cabins have been converted into cozy accommodations with kitchenettes and fjord balconies. Book well in advance for July and August.
