## Driving tips for Patagonia

- **Watch the wind:** Crosswinds can literally push a vehicle off the road or rip car doors backwards off their hinges if opened facing the gale. When exiting the vehicle, hold the door handle with both hands.
- **Ripio (gravel) technique:** While large sections of Ruta 40 are now paved, several stretches in southern Santa Cruz remain unpaved ripio. Reduce speed to 60–70 km/h, brake gently before curves, and maintain a generous following distance behind trucks to avoid shattered windscreens from thrown stones.
- **Fill up at every pump:** In remote steppe towns like Bajo Caracoles or Tres Lagos, fuel deliveries can occasionally be delayed. Never pass a working fuel station without topping off your tank.
