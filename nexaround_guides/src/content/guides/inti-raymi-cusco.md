## The three acts of Inti Raymi

The celebration takes place across three distinct historical locations throughout the day:

1. **Act I: Qorikancha (Temple of the Sun) – 09:00**
   The Sapa Inca (Emperor) emerges from the ancient temple ruins with his imperial court and royal musicians. He praises Inti and calls for the four suyus (quadrants of the empire) to gather. There are no reserved seats here; crowds gather on the surrounding streets and gardens from 06:30.
2. **Act II: Plaza de Armas (Huacaypata) – 11:00**
   The royal retinue is carried on golden palanquins up Calle Loreto into the historic main square. On a raised wooden dais, the Inca meets the modern Mayor of Cusco and offers an invocation in Quechua. Spectators watch from the colonial arched porticos and steps of the cathedral.
3. **Act III: The Esplanade of Sacsayhuamán – 13:45**
   The main ritual occurs on the wide grassy plateau framed by Sacsayhuamán's zigzagging megalithic walls. The ceremony features military drills, offerings of chicha, a symbolic sacrifice of a black llama (no real animal is harmed), and the blessing of new fire.

## Tickets vs free viewing

- **Grandstand tickets at Sacsayhuamán:** EMUFEC sells tiered grandstand seats (Orange, Red, Green). Tickets go on sale each year in early April and typically range from \$120 to \$220 USD. Seats sell out weeks in advance.
- **The free hills (Suchuna and Cruz Moqo):** Thousands of local families and budget travellers sit on the natural rock outcrops overlooking the fortress. Entry to the hills is free, but you must hike up by 08:00 to claim a clear view, and bring sunscreen, water, and warm layers.

## High altitude and practical tips

- **Acclimatise first:** Cusco sits at 3,400 m, and Sacsayhuamán is at 3,700 m. Arrive at least two full days prior to the festival. Drink coca tea and drink plenty of water.
- **Dress in layers:** June mornings are frosty (around 2°C), but midday under the equatorial sun is fierce (up to 22°C with high UV), dropping rapidly to near freezing once the sun dips behind the mountains.
