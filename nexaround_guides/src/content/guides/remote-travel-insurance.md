## The three critical clauses to check

1. **Field Rescue vs Hospital-to-Hospital Evacuation:**
   Standard policies only cover transport *from one hospital to a better hospital*. If you are trapped at a high-altitude pass or along an unpaved desert road, the insurance company will not dispatch a helicopter to the field. Look specifically for **Field Rescue Membership** (such as Global Rescue or Ripcord).
2. **Altitude Limits:**
   Many standard policies void all medical coverage above 2,500 m or 3,000 m. If visiting Cusco (3,400 m), the Pamir Highway (4,655 m), or Ladakh (3,500 m), you must add an **Extreme Altitude / Adventure Sports endorsement**.
3. **Unpaved Road Vehicle Exclusions:**
   Rental car collision damage waivers (CDW) and basic travel insurance often explicitly void coverage if the incident occurs on an unpaved road (gravel, dirt, or sand). Check whether roads like the Dalton, Dempster, or Gibb River Road are specifically covered.

## Satellite SOS and emergency beacons

- Never rely on a phone app when heading into roadless regions. Carry a dedicated two-way satellite communicator such as a **Garmin inReach Mini** or **ZOLEO**.
- Pair your device with a dedicated Search and Rescue (SAR) insurance plan (such as Garmin SAR Insurance underwritten by Tokio Marine HCC), which covers up to \$100,000 in official search-and-rescue operational fees.
