## Dry season vs wet season

- **The Dry Season (May to November):** The salt crust is completely solid, allowing 4x4 vehicles to drive in any direction across the entire flat. Great for optical illusion perspective photography and reaching Isla Incahuasi. Nights are bone-chillingly cold.
- **The Wet Season (December to April):** Water accumulating on the impervious salt creates the famed mirror effect. Driving routes are restricted to the edges to avoid getting vehicles bogged down in brine or damaging vehicle undercarriages with corrosive salt spray.

## Essential expedition tips

- **Altitude preparation:** Starting from Uyuni (3,670 m) or crossing towards the Eduardo Avaroa Reserve (approaching 5,000 m near Laguna Colorada) causes altitude sickness (*soroche*) for unprepared travellers. Acclimatise in Sucre or La Paz first and stay hydrated.
- **Eye protection:** Polarised sunglasses with UV400 protection are non-negotiable. The glare from the white salt at 3,600 m is blinding and can cause rapid snow-blindness.
