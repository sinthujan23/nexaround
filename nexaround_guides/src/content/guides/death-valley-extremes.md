## Extreme heat safety

From May through October, Death Valley experiences life-threatening heat exceeding 45°C daily. If you visit during hot months:

- **The golden water rule:** Carry at least 4 litres (1 gallon) of drinking water per person per day, plus an additional 20 litres in your car trunk for emergencies.
- **Do not hike after 10:00 AM:** High-altitude ridge viewpoints (like Dante's View at 1,669 m) offer cooler breezes, but valley floor hiking in summer can cause heatstroke within thirty minutes.
- **Car breakdowns:** If your vehicle breaks down or gets a flat tire, **always stay with the car**. A car provides shade and is vastly easier for aerial search parties to locate than an individual walking through the desert.
- **No cell service:** Cell reception vanishes across 90% of the park. Download offline maps and consider carrying a satellite messenger (Garmin inReach).
