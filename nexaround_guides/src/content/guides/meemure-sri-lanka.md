## Getting there

**From Colombo:** about 123 km to Kandy (about 3½ hours), then on to Meemure. Allow a full day.

**From Kandy, 78 km, about 3 hours:**

1. Take the **Kandy–Mahiyangana road (A26)** east to **Hunnasgiriya junction**, about 41 km.
2. Turn left onto the mountain road. It climbs past Loolwatte to **Corbett's Gap (Attala Mottuwa)**, a windy pass with wide views over the Knuckles Range.
3. From the pass, the road drops about 12 km into the Meemure valley. This is the slow part: steep, narrow and rough in places. **Use a high-clearance vehicle or a 4x4**, and go slowly after rain.

**Without a car:** a village van is reported to run once a day from Hunnasgiriya junction, around late morning. It's the cheapest way in and a good way to meet people, but the timing isn't reliable: confirm it locally the day before. Otherwise, hire a jeep with a driver in Kandy or at Hunnasgiriya.

**Bring cash from Kandy.** Don't count on an ATM, card payments or reliable phone signal past Hunnasgiriya.

## What to do

- **Climb Lakegala.** The pointed peak above the village is Meemure's landmark. Local legend says that this is where King Ravana's flying machine, the *Dandu Monara*, took off. The climb is steep and exposed near the top: go with a local guide, start early, and turn back if the weather closes in.
- **Walk the paddy terraces** and the paths between farms. Ask before crossing private land.
- **Swim in the river** and visit the waterfalls in the valley. Ask your hosts which pools are safe; water levels rise fast after rain.
- **Stop at Corbett's Gap** on the way in or out for the view over the Knuckles, part of the Central Highlands of Sri Lanka World Heritage Site.

## Where to stay

Don't expect hotels. Visitors stay in **family homestays** and at **campsites** in the village, usually booked by phone or through a contact in Kandy. Expect simple rooms and home-cooked Sri Lankan food, often made with rice and vegetables grown in the valley.

## When to go

The road is at its worst after heavy rain, when the descent from Corbett's Gap can be slippery and streams rise. Check the weather before you go, ask your homestay about the road, and allow an extra day in case you have to wait.

## Visiting with respect

Meemure is a farming village of a few hundred people, not an attraction. A few simple rules keep it that way:

- Dress modestly, and ask before photographing people or homes.
- Keep music off and voices down, especially at night.
- Take every bit of rubbish back out with you.
- Spend your money locally: homestays, guides, food, produce.
