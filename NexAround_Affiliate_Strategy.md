# NexAround — Affiliate Revenue Plan

## How It Works

User discovers a place in NexAround → Taps "Book" → Redirected to partner with our tracking ID → User books → Partner pays NexAround a commission.

NexAround never handles payments. Partners handle everything.

---

## Phase 1 — Quick Integration (1–2 Days)

**What we do:** Sign up for affiliate programs and add tracking IDs to URLs already in the app.

**No app redesign needed.** These buttons and cards already exist:

| Existing Feature | Partner | What User Does | NexAround Earns |
|---|---|---|---|
| Museum Guide → "Skip-the-Line Tickets" button | GetYourGuide (8%) | Books a $35 museum ticket | $2.80 |
| Odyssey Planner → "Find Hotels" card | Booking.com (4–6%) | Books a $350 hotel | $14–$21 |
| Odyssey Planner → "Book Tours" card | Viator (8%) | Books a $75 tour | $6.00 |
| AI Chat (Neva) → "Get a Ride" button | Uber | Requests a ride | Referral credit |
| AI Chat (Neva) → "Find Hotel" card | Booking.com (4–6%) | Books a $200 hotel | $8–$12 |
| Living Map → "Book Nearby" cards | Klook (2–5%) | Books a $60 activity | $1.80 |

**Phase 1 Revenue (at 10K users):** ~$1,300/month

---

## Phase 2 — Click Tracking & Analytics (1 Week)

**What we do:** Add a backend endpoint that logs every affiliate click before redirecting.

This lets us:
- Know which feature earns the most (museum page vs odyssey vs chat)
- Track clicks per user, per partner, per destination
- Show revenue analytics in the admin panel
- Optimize placement based on data

**Phase 2 Revenue (at 10K users):** ~$1,500/month (better optimization)

---

## Phase 3 — Smart AI-Driven Placement (2–3 Weeks)

**What we do:** Make Neva (AI) and the app smarter about when and what to recommend.

| Enhancement | Example |
|---|---|
| Neva suggests bookings in conversation | "Looking for hotels near the Louvre? Here are options on Booking.com" |
| Location-aware partner selection | Show Klook in Asia, GetYourGuide in Europe |
| Post-itinerary booking page | After Odyssey generates a plan → "Book Everything" summary with all links |
| Specific product links | Museum page links to exact ticket (not just search results) |

**Phase 3 Revenue (at 10K users):** ~$2,000+/month (higher conversion)
